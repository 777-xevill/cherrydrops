Hygiene Protocol

Cleaned like
someone's watching

Every console, grip and bench is wiped down on a fixed rotation — not just when it looks dirty. Our floor staff work a documented checklist, and a dedicated quality manager signs it off.

  • Equipment sanitised on rotation — cardio consoles, handles and benches through the day.

  • PPE for cleaning staff — masks, gloves and hair covers as standard practice.

  • Washrooms serviced daily — restocked and deep-cleaned, then checked again at close.

Questions

Before you
walk in

Still Unsure?

Call the front desk — someone on the floor will answer and talk you through it.

01610-021342
Can I visit before joining?

Yes — walk in during opening hours and the front desk will show you the floor. No appointment needed.

Is there a separate ladies' schedule?

Please call the desk on 01610-021342 for current timings and arrangements — we're happy to talk it through.

Do I need my own trainer?

No. Floor coaches are available to all members. One-to-one personal training is an optional add-on if you want dedicated programming.

Where exactly is the gym?

Our main branch is on the 10th floor of BNS Centre, Plot #87, Sector-7, Dhaka–Mymensingh Road, Uttara, Dhaka 1230. We also have a second location at Syed Grand Center, Road #28 Service Road, Sector-7. Take the lift straight up at either.

Is there a prayer space?

Yes — a dedicated, carpeted prayer room on the same floor, open throughout the day.
